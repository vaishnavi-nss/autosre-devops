Pipeline switches

Two safety switches of the correlation engine. Both are at their safe setting unless a person changes them here (needs the access token), and the runner puts them back after every fault run. Changing one restarts the engine, which forgets its recent-incident list. They cannot be changed while a run is in progress.

ML second opinion

Shadow (safe): the learned model gives its opinion, which is recorded and shown, but it never changes a decision.
Gate: if the model disagrees with the rules, the incident is escalated to a person and nothing is run.

Ambiguity rule

Off (safe): if two different causes seem present, the engine acts on the deepest one.
Evaluate: if two known causes' deciding alerts fire together, the engine escalates instead of guessing. Safer with two real faults at once, but it can hand over incidents it could have fixed.
